Kiwizard AI vs. Kiwiform MCP

Continue with the AI Experience That Fits Your Workflow

Kiwizard brings AI into Kiwiform, while MCP brings Kiwiform into the AI assistant you already use.

Kiwiform gives you two distinct ways to work with AI, depending on where you want the experience to happen. Kiwizard, the AI Form Builder inside Kiwiform, keeps you within the Kiwiform platform to create and refine forms with AI. Kiwiform MCP Server takes a different approach by connecting your Kiwiform workspace to a compatible AI assistant, letting you work with Kiwiform through the assistant you already use.
