2. Tally : Lightweight Information Collection for Fast Moving Teams

Create forms through a document-style editing experience that prioritizes simplicity, speed, and ease of use.

Tally has built a strong following among startups, creators, independent businesses, and small teams because of its simple approach to form creation. Instead of relying on traditional drag-and-drop builders, Tally allows users to build forms in a writing environment that feels similar to modern document editors. This makes it easy to launch forms quickly while still supporting logic, payments, signatures, calculations, and data collection workflows. For teams that value speed and simplicity, Tally offers a refreshing alternative to more complex platforms. While Tally performs well for lightweight information collection and straightforward workflows, organizations with more advanced requirements may eventually encounter limitations around customization, design flexibility, visual workflow management, and large-scale operational processes. As information collection becomes more critical to business operations, some teams may require additional control over both the user experience and the systems supporting it.
