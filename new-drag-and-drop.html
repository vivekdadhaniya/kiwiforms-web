Which claims about free plans conflict?

Tally’s branding
Some AI answers say free forms carry no badge. Roundups such as Paperform’s free form builder guide say a badge shows. Confirm on Tally’s pricing page before you publish a branded form.
Jotform’s template count
Jotform’s own templates page says 20,000+. Older answers still repeat “over 10,000” or “hundreds.”
forms.app’s “unlimited”
Its homepage says unlimited responses, but its free plan caps active forms. Some answers say the cap is 5. That figure is reported, not verified.
forms.app’s template count
Comparison videos cite 5,000+ templates. This page has not confirmed that figure on forms.app’s own site, so treat it as unverified.
SurveyJS “100% open-source”
Only the form library is open source. The drag-and-drop Survey Creator needs a paid license.
Prices to quote
Tally’s pricing page showed $24 per month billed monthly for Pro on October 24, 2026. Jotform’s pricing page showed $34 per month billed annually for Bronze on the same date.

How do you customize a form template without code?

  1. 1

    Open your builder’s template library and pick the template closest to your form.

  2. 2

    Drag fields in or out of the canvas, and rename each label to match your wording.

  3. 3

    Add a conditional logic rule, such as showing “Company name” only when “Are you buying for a business?” is Yes.

  4. 4

    Set your colors and fonts, then open the form on your phone to check it reads well.

  5. 5

    Connect a destination such as Google Sheets or Slack, then publish by link, QR code or embed.

Step 1 can also be an AI draft or an imported form in some builders; the plan details for that are in the Kiwiform entry above.

Still deciding?

Answer three questions: where the form will live, how many responses you expect each month, and whether you need payments. Those three answers narrow this list to one or two builders.

Frequently asked questions

Choose by monthly volume first. Under 100 submissions a month and you want a large template library, Jotform’s free plan works; up to 1,000 responses, Fillout’s does; beyond that, pick a builder with no monthly cap, such as Tally or Youform. Then check fit: Cognito Forms if you need calculations and repeating sections and stay under 100 entries, Microsoft Forms if your team already pays for Microsoft 365.

You move to a paid tier, so compare the entry price before you hit the cap: Jotform Bronze is $34 per month billed annually, Fillout Starter $15 per month billed annually, Cognito Forms Pro $19 per month billed annually, and Zoho Forms Basic 13 CAD per month billed annually. If you expect to pass a cap every month, a builder with no monthly cap is usually cheaper. The caveat: uncapped plans still have conditions, such as Tally’s fair-use guidelines and Youform keeping logo upload and custom domains on Pro.
