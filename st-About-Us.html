About Kiwiform

Kiwiform is a modern form builder designed to help you collect better data without complexity. As a free Typeform alternative, it enables conversational forms, smart logic, and unlimited responses, all built to keep data collection simple, flexible, and reliable.

Meet Kiwiform

Kiwiform is a clean and flexible platform for creating forms, surveys, and workflows that feel natural to build and easy to complete. It brings together thoughtful design, powerful logic, and practical customization so creators and teams can collect meaningful information without friction. As a free Typeform alternative, Kiwiform focuses on transparency, usability, and removing limits that slow real work.

Why we built Kiwiform

The Problem

Most form builders start simple but quickly become restrictive. Response limits, locked features, complicated pricing, and cluttered interfaces turn basic data collection into a frustrating experience. Creators are forced to choose between ease of use and real functionality.

The Kiwiform Approach

Kiwiform is being built with adaptability at its core. We are continuously refining how forms are created, managed, and experienced — evolving alongside changing workflows, emerging technologies, and the way people interact online. As the world progresses, we are focusing on keeping data collection clear, flexible, and future-ready, so forms remain useful, accessible, and dependable in real-world scenarios.

What you can do with Kiwiform

Kiwiform brings essential form-building capabilities together in a way that feels intuitive, not overwhelming. Every feature is designed to support real workflows while keeping the experience clean and predictable.

Built for people who collect data every day

Kiwiform is used by creators, freelancers, startups, educators, and teams who need dependable data collection without unnecessary complexity. As a free Typeform alternative, it supports everything from lead generation and feedback to registrations and internal workflows, giving you the flexibility to adapt forms to real situations while keeping the experience simple and consistent.
