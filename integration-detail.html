How to connect kiwiform with slack

Connect SlackIntegration Guide

Connect your form to Slack in 3 simple steps and start receiving form responses where your team works.

Open Connect

Open the Kiwiform form you want to connect, so Form → Connect and select Slack.

Connect Slack Workspace

Authorize your Slack workspace so Kiwiform can connect with it.

Choose Destination

Choose the Slack channel or direct message (DM) to receive real time alerts.

Where responses go

Send form responses to Slack Channels or DMs

Choose where each response should reach your team based on how they work.

Slack Channels

Send responses to a shared channel when the whole team needs to see them.

  • Lead form → Sales channel
  • Support request → Support channel
  • Feedback form → Feedback channel
  • Registration form → Events channel

Direct Messages (DMs)

Send responses to a direct message when one person needs to review or act on them.

  • New lead → Sales rep
  • Application → Recruiter
  • Internal request → Team member

Whether you use a channel or a DM, Kiwiform helps you get each response to the place where it needs attention.
