Why people look for a Typeform alternative

Typeform made conversational forms popular, and it's still a polished product. But the same complaints come up again and again when teams outgrow it:

1. Response caps

Basic includes 100 responses a month and Plus 1,000. A single successful campaign can push you into the next tier.

2. Price climbs quickly

Plans run from Basic to Business (roughly $28 → $91/month as listed), and the Growth plan is $266/month.

3. Branding costs extra

Removing the Typeform badge starts on Plus. Custom domains and custom fonts are Enterprise-only.

4. Compliance is gated

HIPAA, SSO and choosing a US/EU data center are listed as Enterprise-only features.

5. Seats are limited

Basic includes 1 user, Plus 3 and Business 5, so small teams pay more just to collaborate.

6. Free plan is a trial in practice

The free plan is described as a way to "explore" Typeform, with a limited number of monthly responses.

How to switch from Typeform to Kiwiform in about 5 minutes

1

Export your data. In Typeform, open Results → Responses and download a CSV so you keep your history.

2

Import your Typeform. In Kiwiform, create a new form, choose Import Existing Form, select Typeform, paste your public Typeform URL, and click Import Form.

3

Review your imported form. Kiwiform brings over your questions, logic, branding and theme. Recheck everything in the builder and make any final adjustments before publishing.

4

Change your integrations. Reconnect your workflows with Google Sheets, HubSpot, Notion, Airtable, Slack, Zapier, Make or webhooks.
