Why people look for a Jotform alternative

1. Too many caps to track

Forms, monthly submissions, payment submissions, total stored submissions, fields per form and storage are all capped separately on each plan.

2. One user per plan

Starter through Gold are "Individual" plans. Real team access means Enterprise.

3. Payment caps

Registrations with payments hit limits fast: 10 paid submissions a month on free, 100 on Bronze.

4. Busy builder

Thousands of widgets and options make Jotform powerful, but building simple, modern-looking forms takes longer.

5. Old-school form feel

Classic long forms can hurt completion on mobile. Many teams want a conversational, one-question-at-a-time flow.

6. HIPAA starts at Gold

Healthcare teams need Gold ($99/mo) or Enterprise for HIPAA-enabled features.

8. Gravity Forms: WordPress-native

If your site runs on WordPress, Gravity Forms keeps data on your own server with powerful logic and add-ons. Licenses are annual: Basic $59 (1 site), Pro $159 (3 sites, Stripe and Zapier add-ons), Elite $259 (unlimited sites, all add-ons including conversational forms).

Verdict: best for WordPress agencies. Not an option outside WordPress.

9. Wufoo: simple, but dated

Wufoo (by SurveyMonkey) is straightforward, with limits similar to Jotform's: the free plan has 5 forms, 100 entries and 10 fields. Starter is about $16.25/mo billed annually (10 forms, 1,000 entries), and payments start on Professional. Overage is $0.05 per extra entry.

Verdict: fine for basic forms; the builder feels old next to Jotform.

10. Zoho Forms: for Zoho CRM users

Good value inside the Zoho ecosystem. Free: 3 forms, 500 submissions a month. Basic raises you to unlimited forms and 10,000 submissions a month, with payments and approvals on paid tiers.

11. Google Forms: free, unlimited, basic

Jotform vs Wufoo, Formstack, Gravity Forms & Paperform

Jotform vs Wufoo

Their limits are similar at the entry level (5 forms and 100 per month free). Jotform has far more features and templates; Wufoo is simpler but dated and adds per-entry overage fees.

Jotform vs Formstack

Formstack is enterprise-first, with document generation, eSign, Salesforce and HIPAA add-ons, starting at $83/mo. Jotform is cheaper and broader for SMBs.

Jotform vs Gravity Forms

Gravity Forms is a WordPress plugin you self-host, from $59 a year. Jotform is hosted SaaS that works anywhere.

Jotform vs Paperform

Paperform focuses on design and commerce, with no submission caps on paid plans. Jotform has more workflow tools and cheaper entry tiers.
