Pricing

Understand how Kiwiform and Youform compare in cost, limits, and overall value.

Youform

Youform allows unlimited forms and unlimited responses on its free plan, which is rare in the market. A Pro tier is available (around $29/month) for added features like custom domains, removal of branding, and partial submission insights.

Kiwiform

Kiwiform offers transparent pricing with generous limits and advanced features included without confusing tier restrictions. You can start with unlimited forms and responses on the free plan while retaining access to powerful business-ready features — keeping pricing predictable and scalable.

Interface

Compare the user experience and flexibility of both platforms.

Youform

Youform has a modern drag-and-drop interface that makes building forms easy and accessible, even without technical skills. It supports logic, templates, and integrations, but deep layout flexibility and workflow options are limited.

Kiwiform

Kiwiform offers a streamlined builder with support for conversational forms, multi-step layouts, and single-page formats. This flexibility allows you to tailor forms to your audience and business use case without sacrificing ease of use.
